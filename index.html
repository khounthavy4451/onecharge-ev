About OneCharge EV

One Charge New Energy and Technology Sole Co., Ltd. is a Lao company based in Vientiane. Our goal is green energy for transport: fast EV charging stations that help Laos move from imported fuel to clean, home-grown electricity.

  • Official partner in Laos of TELD New Energy and GAC Energy Technology, two leading Chinese charging makers
  • Our own OneCharge EV app – find a charger, top up, charge and pay
  • Payment through Lao banks: BCEL One and LDB
  • Our own team installs, monitors and maintains every charger

What we do

From one charger at a café to a full charging hub – we handle the technology end-to-end.

⚡

Public fast charging

DC fast chargers (120–160 kW per unit, GB/T standard) for cars, open 24 hours a day.

📱

OneCharge EV app

Charging map, live charger status, wallet top-up, charge history – in Lao, English and Chinese.

🤝

Site partnerships

We place chargers at petrol stations, cafés, markets, schools and bus stations, and share the income with the site owner.

🏢

Charging hubs

Group DC chargers from 240 kW up to 960 kW, with up to 24 plugs from one power cabinet – for malls and transport hubs.

🚌

Bus & truck charging

1 MW automatic charging systems for electric buses and freight trucks, for fleets going electric.

🛠️

Installation & care

Site survey, transformer permits, installation, app connection and ongoing maintenance.
